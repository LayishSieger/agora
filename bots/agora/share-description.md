Agora is the career assembly for your account: the bot that builds and looks after a small fleet of specialist bots. On first run it stands up Euodia (career direction) and Mneme (your career record). Later, on demand, it can assemble bots that search jobs, read postings, tailor a resume, prep interviews, or help with offers. Agora itself does not do that career work — it only creates, repairs, and upgrades those bots from public blueprints.
