# ADR 0018 — Euodia is navigator and sole-writes journey.md

Euodia is both career pathfinder and fleet navigator (direction + progress). She sole-writes `/workspace/agora/journey.md` (stage, company, role, paths, timestamps). She creates all career routines, owns career calendar writes, and is the only bot that may connect Gmail/calendar for career work. Stage bots and Mneme do not write journey.md. Two skills stay separate: clarify-direction and navigate-progress.
